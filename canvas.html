Canvas Studio

Set the page size first, like in Photoshop, then place and frame the photos on it. For tall pages such as 8 × 24 in, generative AI can extend the photo to fill the empty space.

Page

2 · Photos
Add photos to the page (several at once), or drop them on the page
3 · Behind the photos

"Blurred photo" fills the empty space with a soft, enlarged copy of the selected photo. It needs no account and nothing is uploaded.

4 · Generative AI fill

Extends the photo into the empty area of the page (for example a half-length portrait on an 8 × 24 in page). The page is sent to Google's Gemini service to be extended, in one or more parts. The photos on the page are not changed: the result goes behind them.

Photos are sent to Google when you press Fill. Use it only with the client's agreement. The key is saved on this device only (in the browser), so each staff device needs its own. Keep the key private and set a spending limit on it.
